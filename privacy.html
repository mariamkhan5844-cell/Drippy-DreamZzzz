Privacy Policy Document

Official user data rules, cookie parameters, and programmatic network compliance records.

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

At Drippy DreamZzzz, accessible from our primary custom web domain, the privacy of our visitors is one of our core parameters. This Privacy Policy blueprint details exactly what information is collected, cataloged, and recorded by our data matrix layers, and how we apply it to protect your ordering transactions.

1. Log File Metrics

Drippy DreamZzzz follows a rigid standard protocol of utilizing server log files. These files index visitors automatically when they navigate open web portals. The data gathered includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, individual browser software profiles, Internet Service Providers (ISPs), date/time stamps, landing/exit vectors, and aggregate click statistics. These metrics are entirely anonymous and are never tied to personally identifiable profiles.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other business platform, Drippy DreamZzzz uses basic cookies to cache specific user preference settings, tracking what page selections visitors access. We utilize this information to optimize your operational usability speeds by customizing storefront content layouts on the fly.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is one of our primary third-party vendor networks. It utilizes DART cookies to serve contextual ad scripts to our platform visitors based on their web travel histories across different domains. You can easily choose to opt-out of DART tracking scripts by visiting the formal Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy directory channels.

4. Programmatic Publisher Partners

Some advertising publishers on our treat network may inject compliance cookies or tracking web beacons. Our primary targeted publisher profile is Google AdSense. Every automated advertising network operates an independent privacy infrastructure page detailing user opt-out rules.

5. Custom Order Form Data Collection

When you fill out an invoice request using our Custom Order Booking Office, we collect specific details including your name, email address, custom event themes, decoration styling choices, and separate treat quantities. This structural data is strictly utilized to process your bakery orders, verify transaction deposit boundaries, and establish direct communication regarding your custom treats. We do not distribute, lease, sell, or rent this order data to third-party organizations.

6. User Consent

By interacting with our artisanal dessert selection channels and using our storefront, you hereby extend formal user consent to our data privacy statement policies and explicitly agree to its legal terms and conditions.
