Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

At Drippy DreamZzz, accessible from our live website, one of our main priorities is the absolute privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document details the specific types of data collected, tracked, and how we protect your personal information.

1. Log Files & Tracking Systems

Drippy DreamZzz follows a standard procedure of using web log files. These files automatically log visitors when they navigate websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and the number of clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used purely to analyze consumer baking trends and administer the layout framework efficiently.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other modern website, Drippy DreamZzz uses standard tracking 'cookies'. These data packages are used to store reader preferences, track which recipe cards visitors accessed, and optimize your web browsing experience by customizing our page layout matching your technical display boundaries.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a verified third-party monetization vendor on our website framework. It utilizes specialized data packets, known as DART cookies, to serve contextual pay-per-click advertisements to our site readers based upon their historical traffic tracks across our site pages and other separate platforms on the internet.

You can choose to manually decline or turn off the use of DART tracking systems by visiting the official Google Ad Network Privacy Policy configuration dashboard.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Drippy DreamZzz's Privacy Policy does not apply to other digital advertisers or external web platforms. Thus, we strongly advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad networks for more detailed operational protocols, as well as instructions about how to opt-out of certain tracking capabilities.

5. Contact Data Information

If you have any remaining questions, require further clarifications, or want more details regarding our cookie processing protocols, please do not hesitate to reach out directly through our dedicated Contact Page form layout.
