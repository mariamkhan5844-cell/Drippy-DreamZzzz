💗 About Us

Founded by Mariam Khan • Since May 2025

Drippy DreamZzzz started in May 2025, founded by Mariam Khan. From our home kitchen in Chicago, we make handmade sweet treats, dipped in chocolate and decorated with love.

Every order is made fresh for birthdays, weddings, and celebrations — Cake Popsicles, Chocolate Covered Oreos, Chocolate Pretzels, Rice Krispies Treats, and Chocolate Strawberries — customised to your theme and colours.

Baked with Love — freshly baked treats made to make you smile! Chicago & suburbs only. Minimum 12 per flavour. Pickup is free, and delivery is calculated from your address: within 5 miles $10, over 5 miles $20.
