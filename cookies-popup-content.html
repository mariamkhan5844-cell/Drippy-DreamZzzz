1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are minute alphanumeric text structures downloaded straight to your network devices when you browse standard online portals. They act as automated system memory trackers, enabling platforms to maintain secure user state balances, recall user preferences, and support third-party application plug-ins smoothly.

2. How We Apply Cookies & Local Cache

Our ordering platform deploys session tracking arrays and persistent data blocks across two distinct operations:

  • Fulfillment Performance: We map your separate selection quantities and custom theme data onto local string scripts so your shopping cart totals don't refresh or erase as you cross directory paths.
  • Programmatic Customizations: Third-party network publishers utilize cookie keys to analyze traffic trends and display relevant contextual advertisements matching your personal interests.
